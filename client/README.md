# SunPower Solutions – Operations Client

React + Vite frontend for the SunPower Solutions Solar Installation Management System (Odoo x LPU Hackathon 2026).

## Scope & Compliance Rules
- **Branding**: SunPower Solutions.
- **Capacity**: Only 1 kW to 5 kW inclusive (no arbitrary step restrictions).
- **Technician Roster**: 15 technicians (represented by clearly labeled synthetic demo records).
- **Operating Scale**: Approximately 40 rooftop projects per month benchmark (target scale, not actual historical performance).
- **Customer Base**: Homes and small factories.
- **Warranty Terms**:
  - Solar Panels: 20 years
  - Inverter: 2 years
  - Battery: 2 years
  - Charge Controller: Not specified in the official problem statement.
  - Warranty begins on completed delivery date.
- **Packages**:
  - Solar Panel Set: panels, connectors, wires.
  - On-Grid: inverter, wiring boxes, battery charge controller, panels, safety earthing, metal stands.
  - Off-Grid: all On-Grid components + large battery.
  - Installation: separate service (fitting and testing at customer site).
